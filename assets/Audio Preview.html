Original sound set

All eight tracks were synthesized from scratch — no third-party samples, no Mario audio, nothing sourced. That makes them inherently free of outside copyright (effectively public-domain / CC0, with no attribution required). Tap to audition.

Format  16-bit PCM WAV @ 44.1 kHz, in assets/audio/. Bevy's audio backend (rodio) plays WAV directly — enable the wav feature on the bevy dependency. To match the repo's existing .ogg loader instead, convert with ffmpeg -i in.wav out.ogg.
